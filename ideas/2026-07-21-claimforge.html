SaaS Idea Log — 2026-07-21

ClaimForge

AI turns a public insurance adjuster's photos, scope notes, and Xactimate estimates into a carrier-ready settlement demand package in minutes instead of the 5–15 hours it takes by hand.

Target Customer

Independent and public insurance adjusters handling residential property claims (fire, water, wind, hail) — roughly 10,000+ licensed public adjusters in the US, most operating solo or in shops of 2–5 people, paid on a percentage of the settlement they win for the policyholder.

Pain Point

Turning field photos and a damage scope into a persuasive, well-documented demand package is still a manual slog: pulling line items out of Xactimate, writing narrative justification, citing comparable settlements and applicable policy/building-code provisions, and assembling it into a format each carrier will actually engage with. It routinely eats 5–15 hours per claim. Because adjusters are paid a percentage of the settlement — not by the hour — every hour spent formatting is an hour not spent on the next claim, and a rushed or thin package directly lowers what the carrier offers. There's no dedicated tool for this; adjusters cobble it together in Xactimate exports, Word, and Excel.

MVP Workflow

  1. Adjuster uploads phone photos of the damage, their field scope notes, and the prior carrier estimate or their own Xactimate export (ESX) for the claim.
  2. Document AI extracts and normalizes damage line items, matching photos to specific scope entries.
  3. The system cross-references line items against a regional repair-cost database to flag under-scoped or missing items versus typical settlements for that damage type and geography.
  4. AI drafts a formatted demand letter and package: narrative summary, itemized damages with photo citations, applicable policy/code references, and comparable-settlement context.
  5. Adjuster reviews and edits the draft in-app, then exports a polished PDF/DOCX package ready to send to the carrier.
  6. Package and claim status are tracked so the adjuster can see turnaround time and follow up before the negotiation stalls.

Data & Integrations Needed

Xactimate ESX file parsing Regional repair-cost database Document AI / OCR for carrier estimates & policies State insurance code & building code reference library Photo-to-line-item matching PDF/DOCX generation

Pricing Model

Per-claim fee (~$49–99/claim) or a monthly subscription (~$199–499/mo for a bundle of claims), priced against the adjuster's own commission upside rather than against generic document-SaaS comparables — a package that measurably raises a settlement easily justifies a two-to-three-figure cost on a claim worth tens of thousands.

First Version Scope

Single vertical (residential water and fire damage) in a single high-volume state to start (e.g. Florida). Photo + PDF/ESX ingestion, template-based demand-package generation, manual review before sending — no direct carrier-portal integration in v1. Target: 15–20 pilot public adjusters recruited through state public-adjuster associations and CE/license-renewal course networks.

What Makes It Non-Obvious

Most "AI for insurance" plays chase the carrier side (claims triage, fraud detection) — crowded, enterprise-sales-heavy territory. Public adjusters are the underserved counterparty: solo operators with real, calculable ROI (a better package can mean thousands more per claim) but no tooling built for them specifically. The differentiator isn't generic document drafting — it's proprietary ESX/Xactimate parsing plus a package format tuned to what actually moves carrier settlement offers, sold through the adjuster's own trade associations and license-renewal channels rather than typical SaaS PLG motions.
