LapLedger

Field-photo AI that turns a commercial roofing crew's daily phone pictures into a manufacturer-ready warranty submittal, so contractors stop losing retainage and approved-installer status to missing seam probes, fastener counts and pre-install notices.

Target Customer

Small-to-mid commercial roofing contractors (10–80 field staff, $3M–$30M revenue) who install single-ply (TPO, PVC, EPDM) and modified-bitumen systems under manufacturer system warranties (GAF, Carlisle, Holcim/Elevate, Johns Manville, Sika Sarnafil). Buyer is the owner or operations/project manager; daily users are foremen and the office warranty/closeout coordinator.

Pain Point

On commercial jobs the building owner's spec almost always requires a 15–30 year manufacturer NDL (no-dollar-limit) warranty, and the general contractor holds 5–10% retainage until that warranty is issued. To get it, the roofer must file a pre-installation notice on time, follow the manufacturer's exact fastening pattern for the wind zone, probe and document every heat-welded seam, log daily weather and adhesive temperatures, and pass a manufacturer technical inspection. That evidence lives in foremen's camera rolls, paper daily logs and scattered texts. When the inspector flags a missing item (no photo of the corner enhancement fastening, seam-probe log gaps, the wrong membrane lot recorded), the warranty slips by weeks, a crew goes back to the roof, and retainage on a $600k job ($30k–$60k) sits unpaid. Repeated misses can cost the contractor its approved-installer tier, and with it access to the best bids. Nobody on the team owns closeout paperwork full-time, so it gets done badly and late.

MVP Workflow

  1. The PM creates a job: manufacturer, roof system, warranty length, wind-uplift rating and roof size. LapLedger loads that manufacturer's requirement checklist for the system, covering pre-install notice deadlines, required photo shots, fastener density by roof zone (field, perimeter, corners) and inspection triggers.
  2. LapLedger immediately calculates and calendars the pre-installation notice deadline and drafts the notice from job data for one-click submission to the manufacturer's portal or rep email.
  3. Foremen use a lightweight mobile capture flow, not a full app: each day a guided shot list ("NW corner fastening, seam probe section 4, drain detail #2") is texted to them. Photos are auto-tagged with GPS, time and the roof-zone grid.
  4. Vision AI checks every photo against what it was meant to show. It counts plates per board against the zone's required density, detects un-probed or fish-mouthed seams, reads membrane roll labels for lot numbers, and flags blurry or mislabeled shots while the crew is still on the roof.
  5. Daily weather is pulled automatically for the job address and cross-checked against adhesive and membrane temperature limits; out-of-range days are flagged with a note to document mitigation.
  6. At substantial completion, LapLedger assembles a submittal packet in the manufacturer's expected order (notice, daily logs, photo index by roof zone, material lot list, punch-list sign-offs) and shows a readiness score before the technical inspection is requested.
  7. After inspection, punch items are logged against the roof grid, assigned to the foreman with re-shoot requirements, and the warranty issuance date and retainage release are tracked to closure.

Data & Integrations Needed

Manufacturer warranty & installation spec manuals (parsed into checklists) FM Global / ASCE 7 wind-zone fastening tables Vision models for fastener counting, seam & label reading Historical + daily weather API (NOAA / Open-Meteo) SMS capture links (Twilio) with photo EXIF/GPS Roof plan PDF upload for zone grid overlay Later: Procore, Buildertrend, AccuLynx, CompanyCam import

Pricing Model

Per-active-job pricing that tracks the contractor's revenue: $149 per warranted job up to 50,000 sq ft and $299 for larger jobs, or a $499/mo plan covering 6 concurrent jobs, then $59 per extra job. The ROI pitch is simple: one avoided return trip (~$2,500 in crew and lift costs) or two weeks' faster release of $40k in retainage pays for a year. Optional add-on: $99 per job for a human-reviewed "inspection-ready" packet check before the manufacturer rep visits.

First Version Scope

Two manufacturers (GAF and Carlisle, the largest commercial single-ply share) and TPO/EPDM mechanically attached and fully adhered systems only. SMS-link photo capture as a web app (no native app), fastener-count and seam-probe photo checks, weather cross-checks, a pre-install notice calculator and a PDF submittal packet generator. No direct manufacturer portal integration yet; the packet is downloaded and uploaded by the coordinator. Sell through regional roofing contractor associations, manufacturer territory reps (who also hate incomplete submittals), and roofing-industry Facebook and Reddit groups, starting with 10 design-partner contractors in high-wind states (FL, TX, Carolinas).

What Makes It Non-Obvious

Construction photo tools like CompanyCam are crowded, but they store photos; they don't know what a given manufacturer's warranty requires or whether a photo actually proves it. The value isn't "AI on the jobsite" in general. It sits in one painful document gate with direct cash attached: retainage that can't be released until a manufacturer issues a warranty. That makes ROI unusually easy to prove, unlike generic productivity software. The manufacturer requirement library is boring, manual and compounding work that becomes the moat, and the fastener-counting vision task is narrow enough to be very accurate with modest training data. There's also a hidden second buyer: manufacturers' technical services teams and territory reps spend huge amounts of time rejecting bad submittals, so a standardized, pre-validated packet format could later be sold or co-marketed to them, turning a contractor tool into a channel the manufacturers themselves promote to their approved-installer networks.
