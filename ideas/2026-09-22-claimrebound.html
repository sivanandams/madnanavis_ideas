ClaimRebound

AI reads an insurance denial letter for a physical therapy, chiropractic, or OT clinic, matches it against a structured library of payer medical-necessity policies, and drafts a citation-backed appeal in minutes instead of the 45+ it takes a biller by hand.

Target Customer

Independent physical therapy, chiropractic, and occupational therapy clinics with 1–5 locations that have no dedicated billing/appeals department — the front-desk person or owner-clinician currently does appeals between patients, or simply doesn't file them.

Pain Point

These clinics see denial rates of 10–20% on claims (exceeded visit caps, "not medically necessary," missing modifiers, documentation mismatches). Fighting a denial means reading the payer's specific medical policy or LCD, matching it against the chart note, and writing a letter — 30–60 minutes of specialized work most clinics don't have staff time for. Industry estimates put unappealed, recoverable denials at $50K–$150K/year for a typical multi-location practice — money that's rightfully owed but simply written off because filing is too slow and too specialized.

MVP Workflow

  1. Clinic uploads the denial letter/EOB (PDF or fax image) and the relevant chart/SOAP notes for that date of service.
  2. OCR and document parsing extract the payer, denial reason code, CPT codes, and date of service.
  3. The denial reason is matched against a maintained library of payer-specific medical-necessity policies and LCDs to find the exact clause the payer is applying.
  4. The system drafts an appeal letter that cites the matched policy language, pulls supporting facts from the chart note, and flags any correctable errors (wrong modifier, missing units, mismatched diagnosis code).
  5. Clinic staff review, edit, and send the letter (download, e-fax, or payer portal paste) — no auto-submission in v1.
  6. Outcome (paid, denied again, partial) is logged against the claim to keep improving the policy-match library over time.

Data & Integrations Needed

OCR for denial letters/EOBs Public CMS LCD/NCD policy text Commercial payer medical policy documents EHR/billing exports (WebPT, TheraOffice, Cliniko) Structured denial-reason-to-policy mapping engine E-fax API for outbound appeals

Pricing Model

$19–$25 per appeal generated, or a monthly plan ($149–$399/mo by clinic size) with a volume allowance — priced against the staff time it replaces, not as a generic subscription. A contingency option (15–20% of recovered revenue) can be offered to larger multi-location groups who want zero upfront cost.

First Version Scope

Manual upload only, no EHR integration. Cover the top 5 commercial payers plus Medicare in one state, and only the most common PT/chiro denial reasons (medical necessity, visit-cap exceeded, missing modifier). Output is a downloadable draft letter, not auto-submission. Distribute through state PT and chiropractic associations and billing-focused Facebook/LinkedIn groups where clinic owners already trade denial horror stories.

What Makes It Non-Obvious

The moat isn't the letter-writing — any LLM can draft a plausible appeal. It's turning each payer's dense, unstructured medical-policy PDF into a queryable rule engine keyed to specific denial codes, something a generic chatbot wrapper doesn't do and a competitor would have to painstakingly rebuild payer by payer. Small PT/chiro clinics are also underserved on purpose: enterprise RCM platforms target hospitals and large groups, leaving solo and small-group clinics — who need this just as badly but can't afford enterprise contracts — with only "hire more billing staff" as their option today.
