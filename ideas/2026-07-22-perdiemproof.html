SaaS Idea Log — 2026-07-22

PerDiemProof

AI cross-checks travel-nurse contracts and pay stubs against GSA per-diem tables — catching shorted stipends and tax-home audit risk before they cost thousands.

Target Customer

Travel nurses and travel allied-health professionals (radiology techs, surgical techs, physical therapists) working 8–13 week contracts through staffing agencies like Aya, Cross Country, AMN, Fusion, and Medical Solutions.

Pain Point

Travel healthcare pay is split into a taxable "bill rate" wage plus non-taxable stipends (housing, meals & incidentals) that must track GSA per-diem tables for the assignment's exact ZIP/county and dates, plus IRS tax-home rules. Agencies routinely miscalculate this — wrong locality, stale rate year, blended-rate math errors — shorting travelers hundreds to thousands of dollars per contract, or structuring stipends in ways that jeopardize tax-home status and expose the traveler to a five-figure IRS liability if audited. Verifying it requires either a travel-tax specialist CPA (rare, $300+/session) or manually cross-referencing dense GSA PDFs against contract fine print — something almost no traveler actually does before signing.

MVP Workflow

  1. Traveler uploads or forwards their agency contract (PDF/photo) showing bill rate, stipend breakdown, assignment location, and dates.
  2. Document AI extracts base rate, stipend categories and amounts, assignment ZIP/county, and contract start/end dates.
  3. System pulls the current GSA per-diem lookup for that locality and date range and computes the maximum allowable non-taxable stipend.
  4. A diff engine flags any contract stipend below the GSA max (a correctable underpayment) and any structuring pattern that risks tax-home status (e.g., no duplicated-expense trail, stipend exceeding safe-harbor norms), producing a plain-English risk report.
  5. Once working, the traveler forwards each pay stub; the system reconciles actual pay against the traveler's own signed contract terms and flags the specific stub line that doesn't match.
  6. For any flagged issue, the system drafts a ready-to-send message to the recruiter citing the exact contract clause and GSA reference to request a correction.

Data & Integrations Needed

Document AI for contract & pay-stub parsing GSA per-diem public dataset (CONUS lookup by ZIP/date) Email-forward ingestion Contract-template library for top agencies Rules engine for tax-home risk checks PDF/report generation

Pricing Model

Flat fee per contract review (~$19–29 per 8–13 week assignment) plus an optional $9/mo add-on for ongoing paycheck reconciliation during an active assignment — priced low enough to buy on the spot when a traveler is about to sign, with the subscription as pure upside once trust is established.

First Version Scope

Support contract formats from the 5 largest travel-nursing agencies and GSA CONUS per-diem lookup only (no OCONUS/foreign assignments). Ship the contract-level stipend and tax-home risk report first; defer ongoing pay-stub reconciliation to v2. Manual upload only, no direct agency integrations. Distribute through r/TravelNursing, travel-nurse Facebook groups, and travel-nurse influencers/podcasts as the initial channel.

What Makes It Non-Obvious

Travel-healthcare tech investment has gone almost entirely into job-matching marketplaces (Vivian, Trusted, IntelyCare) — nobody is building for the contract-math verification problem, even though the core data source (GSA per-diem tables) is public, structured, and perfectly suited to deterministic cross-referencing rather than generic chatbot Q&A. The tax-home audit-risk framing also raises willingness to pay well past a typical "did I get paid right" checker: this isn't just recovering a shorted stipend, it's protection against a personal IRS liability, which is a fundamentally higher-stakes pitch for the same underlying automation.
