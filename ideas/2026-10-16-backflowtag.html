BackflowTag

Photo-to-compliance-report automation for certified backflow preventer testers: snap the gauge and device tag, and get the correct test report filed with the right water utility, with next year's due dates tracked automatically.

Target Customer

Solo and 2–5 person certified backflow assembly testers (often plumbers or irrigation contractors holding a state/utility tester certification) who test commercial, irrigation, and fire-line backflow preventers — tens of thousands of testers in the US, each running 500–3,000 devices a year.

Pain Point

Every device must be tested annually and the result reported to the local water utility, which has its own form, portal, or emailed-PDF format and its own deadlines. Testers do the 20-minute test, then spend 10–15 minutes per device on handwriting, retyping gauge readings, finding the right utility's portal, and uploading. A missed or rejected report means the customer gets a shutoff-threat notice and blames the tester. Customer reminders are tracked in spreadsheets and paper tag cards, so repeat business leaks every year.

MVP Workflow

  1. Tester photographs the device data plate and the test kit's gauge readout on site (or types readings in a fast mobile form).
  2. OCR extracts make, model, serial, size, location and test pressures; the tester confirms pass/fail and any repairs made.
  3. The app maps the service address to its water utility and renders the report in that utility's required format.
  4. The report is submitted via the utility's portal, email address, or upload form (assisted in v1), and a copy goes to the property owner.
  5. Device record is stored with its history; the app schedules next-year reminders and sends branded booking links to the owner 60 days before the due date.
  6. Dashboard shows overdue devices, rejected reports, and rebooking revenue.

Data & Integrations Needed

Mobile OCR for data plates Water-utility format & contact directory Address-to-utility geocoding Tester certification & calibration records PDF generation & email/SMS QuickBooks invoicing (later)

Pricing Model

$49–99/month per tester with a device allowance, plus ~$1 per submission above it. Optional add-on: a 15% fee on rebookings generated by the automated owner reminders. Priced against the 10+ minutes saved per device and the annual repeat revenue protected.

First Version Scope

One metro area and its 5–10 largest water utilities. Mobile web app with photo OCR, 3–5 utility report templates, PDF output with manual submit (download/email), device history, and annual reminders by email/SMS. Recruit 15 testers via local plumbing/irrigation associations and backflow-certification training courses. No portal automation in v1.

What Makes It Non-Obvious

General field-service tools (Jobber, ServiceTitan) don't know that the deliverable is a regulator-facing report in a different format for every utility, and the utility directory plus format library is the real moat. It looks like a tiny, boring niche, but each utility format learned makes the product better for every other tester in that service area. The reminder loop converts a compliance chore into recurring revenue for the tester, which is what makes them pay and stay.
