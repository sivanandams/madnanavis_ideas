SaaS Idea Log — 2026-07-15

ClaimSnap

AI turns your before/after turnover photos into insurance-ready damage claims in minutes — so short-term rental hosts stop quietly eating the cost of guest damage.

Target Customer

Airbnb / Vrbo hosts and small property-management companies operating 5–200 furnished short-term rental units — especially self-managed hosts and boutique PM shops with no dedicated ops or claims staff.

Pain Point

Platforms, security-deposit services (Superhog, Truvi, CSA), and travel insurers all require itemized evidence — before/after photos, receipts, market-price comps — submitted within a tight 14–30 day window after checkout. Most hosts have no organized "move-in" baseline photos, don't know fair replacement costs, and burn hours assembling claims that still get rejected on a technicality. It's a recurring $50–500 loss per incident, multiplied across every turnover a busy host or PM company runs each year.

MVP Workflow

  1. Host connects their calendar/PMS (Guesty, Hostaway, OwnerRez) or logs turnovers manually.
  2. Baseline "move-in" photos are captured per room through a simple mobile flow (or pulled automatically from a smart lock/camera at turnover, if connected).
  3. After each stay, the cleaner or host uploads "post-checkout" photos through the same flow.
  4. Vision AI diffs each before/after room pair, flags likely damage (stains, breaks, missing items) with a confidence score.
  5. Host confirms flagged items; AI pulls a repair/replacement cost estimate from a maintained pricing reference and drafts an itemized claim + demand letter formatted for the Airbnb Resolution Center, the relevant insurer, or the deposit platform.
  6. Host submits with one click (or copy/paste in v1) and the claim is tracked through to resolution.

Data & Integrations Needed

Calendar/PMS APIs Mobile photo capture + EXIF verification Vision model for damage detection Repair/replacement cost dataset Smart lock/camera (optional) PDF/e-sign generation

Pricing Model

Low monthly base per property (~$15–25/property/mo) plus an optional success fee (~10%) on damage amounts actually recovered — aligning incentives the way recovery-style services typically do. A free tier capped at one property drives bottom-up adoption among individual hosts before expanding into PM companies.

First Version Scope

Single-property, manual-turnover mode: mobile web app for before/after photo upload, AI diff detection covering the top five damage categories (stains/burns, broken furniture, missing items, wall damage, appliance damage), an auto-generated PDF claim packet with cost estimates from a starter pricing catalog, and manual submission to the platform (no direct Airbnb API auto-submit yet — that requires business API access). Target: 20–30 pilot hosts recruited from STR host Facebook groups and subreddits.

What Makes It Non-Obvious

"AI for short-term rentals" almost always means guest-messaging chatbots or dynamic pricing — both already crowded (Hospitable, PriceLabs, Wheelhouse). Nobody is targeting the unglamorous, high-frequency, financially painful moment of turnover damage documentation. It's exactly the kind of structured, verifiable, deadline-bound task vision AI is genuinely good at — comparing two photo sets and pricing a repair — rather than another conversational layer bolted onto an existing dashboard.
