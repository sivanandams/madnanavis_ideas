Reclaimly

Point a phone at an appliance's nameplate and Reclaimly tells an independent repair tech whether the job is still under manufacturer warranty — then fills out and files the claim for them.

Target Customer

Independent appliance and HVAC repair technicians and small repair shops (1–10 techs) doing in-home service calls for washers, dryers, refrigerators, dishwashers, and residential HVAC units — not affiliated with a manufacturer's authorized service network.

Pain Point

A meaningful share of the units techs service are still covered by a manufacturer's standard or extended warranty, but techs routinely skip filing the claim and just bill the homeowner instead. Every manufacturer has its own claim portal, its own labor-rate caps, its own required fields (serial number, purchase-date verification, fault code, parts used), and claims get rejected for small omissions. Filing one properly eats 20–40 minutes of unpaid admin time with no guarantee of payment, so a tech who's already booked solid simply treats the unit as out-of-warranty and charges the customer full price — leaving $150–$800 of legitimate manufacturer reimbursement on the table per eligible job, multiplied across dozens of jobs a month.

MVP Workflow

  1. Tech photographs the appliance's nameplate/rating plate and briefly describes the fault in the Reclaimly mobile app.
  2. A vision model extracts the manufacturer, model number, serial number, and manufacture date from the photo.
  3. Reclaimly checks the serial number and manufacture date against known warranty-term rules and, where available, the manufacturer's own serial-based coverage lookup, and returns an instant covered / not covered / needs proof-of-purchase verdict.
  4. If covered, the app pre-fills that manufacturer's specific claim form (fault code, parts, labor, customer/unit details) from the job data already entered; the tech reviews and submits with one tap.
  5. Reclaimly tracks each claim's status (submitted → under review → paid/denied) on a dashboard and nudges the tech when a manufacturer requests additional documentation.
  6. Denied claims get a one-click "resubmit with correction" flow that highlights exactly which field triggered the rejection.

Data & Integrations Needed

Vision/OCR model for nameplate reading Manufacturer warranty-term rules database (top 6–8 brands) Manufacturer serial-to-coverage lookup APIs/portals where available Headless form-fill automation for manufacturer claim portals Mobile photo capture (PWA) Light job/customer tracking + status dashboard Optional: Housecall Pro / ServiceTitan job import

Pricing Model

Success fee of 10–15% of the manufacturer reimbursement, charged only when a claim is actually paid — zero cost to try. Optional $29–49/mo tier adds unlimited claim submissions with priority processing and the dashboard/reporting view once a shop is filing high volume.

First Version Scope

Support the 5–8 highest-volume residential appliance and HVAC manufacturers (e.g., Whirlpool, GE, Samsung, LG, Trane, Carrier). Ship semi-automated submission first — Reclaimly pre-fills the correct manufacturer form and the tech reviews and clicks submit, rather than a fully autonomous portal bot. No factoring/cash-advance feature in v1; that's a v2 add-on once claim volume and payout timing are well understood.

What Makes It Non-Obvious

Nearly every tool built for repair techs targets scheduling and dispatch (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro) — a crowded field. Almost nobody targets the revenue quietly leaking out of unfiled warranty claims, because it sits at the unglamorous intersection of computer vision, a patchwork of manufacturer bureaucracies, and a customer base that is notoriously tool-fatigued and not chatbot-friendly. A success-fee model — paid only when the tech gets paid — removes the adoption friction that kills most tools aimed at busy solo technicians, and turns what looks like "another appliance app" into a pure-upside revenue-recovery service.
