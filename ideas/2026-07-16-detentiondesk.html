DetentionDesk

AI turns ELD timestamps and rate confirmations into airtight detention-pay claims — so owner-operators stop eating thousands a year in unpaid dock wait time.

Target Customer

Owner-operators and small trucking fleets (1–25 trucks) hauling dry van, reefer, or flatbed freight for brokers and shippers under rate confirmations that include detention clauses.

Pain Point

Detention time — waiting past free time at pickup or delivery — is one of trucking's largest uncompensated costs; industry estimates put unclaimed detention pay in the billions annually. To collect it, a driver has to cross-reference ELD "on-duty-not-driving" timestamps against free-time and detention-rate terms buried in each broker's rate confirmation (every broker's contract language and format differs), then file a claim within a tight 24–72 hour window in the exact format that broker demands. Most owner-operators have no back-office staff to do this consistently, so claims are skipped, filed late, or rejected on a technicality — a recurring $100–400 loss per detained load.

MVP Workflow

  1. Driver connects their ELD (Motive, Samsara, KeepTruckin/Verizon Connect) or forwards ELD log PDFs for a load.
  2. Driver uploads or forwards the rate confirmation (photo or email) for the same load.
  3. Document AI extracts the free-time window, detention rate, and any threshold/notice clauses from the rate-con — handling the fact that every broker's template reads differently.
  4. The system cross-references ELD stop timestamps against BOL check-in/check-out times to compute exact detention hours owed, no manual math.
  5. It auto-drafts a claim packet — timestamps, hours, rate, supporting document excerpts — formatted to match that specific broker's submission requirements, and starts a countdown alert against the filing deadline.
  6. Driver reviews, edits if needed, and submits with one click (or copy/paste in v1); the claim is tracked to payment with automated follow-up reminders.

Data & Integrations Needed

ELD APIs (Motive, Samsara, Verizon Connect) Document AI for rate-con & BOL parsing Email-forward ingestion Broker claim-format template library Time-series matching engine PDF generation & e-sign

Pricing Model

Per-truck monthly subscription (~$29–49/truck/mo) with an optional success-fee-only tier (~15% of recovered detention pay, no monthly cost) for drivers wary of upfront spend — mirroring how recovery-style services typically price to align incentive with payout.

First Version Scope

Manual, load-by-load flow with no ELD API in v1: driver uploads a photo/PDF of the ELD log excerpt plus the rate confirmation for a single load. Rate-con parsing tuned to the 8–10 largest freight brokers' contract formats. Auto-calculated detention claim as a formatted PDF, plus a filing-window countdown reminder by text/email. Target: 20–30 pilot owner-operators recruited from trucking Facebook groups, r/Truckers, and OOIDA-adjacent communities.

What Makes It Non-Obvious

"AI for trucking" almost always means load-matching marketplaces or dispatch chatbots — already crowded territory (DAT, Truckstop, and a wave of AI dispatch copilots). Nobody is targeting the tedious, deadline-bound, contract-clause-dependent paperwork of detention claims, even though it's exactly the kind of task modern AI is genuinely suited for: extracting variable terms from inconsistent contract documents and running deterministic time-series math against them, not holding a conversation. And because it recovers money already owed under an existing signed contract, the value is immediate and provable on day one — not a speculative efficiency gain.
