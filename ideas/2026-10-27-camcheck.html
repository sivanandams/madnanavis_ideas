CAMCheck

AI reads a small retail tenant's lease and the landlord's annual CAM reconciliation, finds overcharges, and files the audit request before the lease's audit-rights window closes.

Target Customer

Independent restaurants, salons, clinics and small multi-location retailers (1–15 units) leasing space in shopping centers or office buildings under NNN or modified-gross leases, with no in-house finance or real-estate staff. Secondary channel: bookkeepers and fractional CFOs serving these businesses.

Pain Point

Every year the landlord sends a Common Area Maintenance (CAM), tax and insurance reconciliation: a dense spreadsheet that often arrives with a surprise bill of several thousand dollars. Leases usually give the tenant only 60–180 days to dispute it, and that right is lost silently if missed. Common errors include a wrong pro-rata share after vacancies, uncapped "controllable" expenses that should be capped, management fees charged on pass-throughs, capital items billed as repairs, and costs the lease explicitly excludes. Lease-audit firms exist but work on 30–40% contingency and only take large accounts; small tenants just pay.

MVP Workflow

  1. Tenant uploads the lease PDF (plus amendments) and the annual reconciliation statement; the statement date is entered or detected.
  2. Document AI extracts lease terms: pro-rata share definition, base year, caps and escalators, exclusions, gross-up clause, management-fee limit, audit window and notice procedure.
  3. It parses the reconciliation line items into a normalized ledger and runs deterministic checks against the extracted terms (share math, cap breaches, excluded categories, fee-on-fee, year-over-year jumps).
  4. Each finding shows the lease clause cited, the statement line, and the estimated dollar overcharge, with a confidence rating. Ambiguous clauses are flagged for human review rather than asserted.
  5. A countdown starts for the audit-request deadline, with reminders. One click generates a notice-compliant audit request letter and a document-request list (invoices, GL detail), formatted to the lease's notice rules.
  6. Tenant sends it by their own email or certified mail; the app tracks the landlord's response and the later credit or refund.

Data & Integrations Needed

Lease and reconciliation PDFs (user upload) Document AI / table extraction Clause-to-rule library (caps, exclusions, gross-up) Deterministic CAM math engine Deadline engine with email/SMS alerts Letter templates with state notice rules Optional: QuickBooks export for payment history

Pricing Model

Free scan that shows the number of potential findings and total estimated dollars, with details unlocked for a $149 one-time fee per reconciliation, or $29/mo per location for deadline tracking across multiple leases and years. A success-fee option (15% of recovered dollars, capped) is available for tenants who prefer no upfront cost. Bookkeeper plan: $199/mo for up to 25 client locations.

First Version Scope

US retail NNN leases only, English, single-property reconciliations. Ship lease-term extraction, the six most common error checks and the deadline countdown, with a templated audit letter. Skip landlord negotiation, legal advice and office or industrial leases for now. Validate with 20–30 tenants and a handful of local lease-audit consultants, whose past findings can be used to calibrate the checks. Go to market through restaurant associations, small business Facebook and Reddit groups, and bookkeepers.

What Makes It Non-Obvious

The value comes from the combination of a long, unstructured lease and a messy annual statement, and a ticking contractual deadline most tenants don't know they have. The core engine is rule-based arithmetic against extracted clauses, not chat, so findings are checkable and defensible. Incumbent audit firms are economically unable to serve a tenant with a $3,000 dispute; software reduces the cost per audit to near zero, opening an underserved long tail. Every recovered dollar is a clear ROI story, and each location generates recurring annual demand.
